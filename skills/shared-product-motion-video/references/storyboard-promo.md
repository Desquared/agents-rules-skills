# Template: 40 s product promo

Ten scenes, modelled beat for beat on the reference promo. Times are film seconds; each scene's window
overlaps the next by 0.3 s (the dissolve). "Beats" are scene-local seconds (0 = the scene's start).
Replace every bracketed item with the product's real content. Drop a proof scene rather than shorten
all of them: seven scenes at full length beat ten rushed ones.

## Arc

**Problem (struck out) -> product -> six proofs, one per scene -> outcome -> end card.**

## Rules this template encodes

- The hook states the **problem as the viewer would say it**, then corrects it by hand (the strike).
- The brand appears by 4.5 s, and again at the end.
- Each proof is **one claim + one real demonstration + one mark**. The caption is readable before the
  action starts.
- Every number is real and counted up from zero once.
- The film ends on a still end card, not a fade to black.
